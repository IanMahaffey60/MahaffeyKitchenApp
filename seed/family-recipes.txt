=== Whiskey Peppercorn Steak | American | 2 | 30 | celebration | 00
1 tbsp green peppercorns (or peppercorn medley)
3 tsp fresh cracked black peppercorns
1/2 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup whiskey
2 beef steaks, 1 to 1 1/2 inch thick (top sirloin)
1 tsp kosher salt
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp butter
---
Marinate the steak with olive oil and press on kosher salt and cracked black pepper. Let it sit for 1 hour or more.
Heat a heavy skillet to a high temperature. Sear the steak quickly, about 20 seconds per side, then grill to the doneness you like.
While the steak grills, deglaze the pan and add a little butter, then the cracked black pepper, green peppercorns, heavy cream and whiskey. Cook over medium heat until reduced by half.
Put the steak on a serving dish and pour the sauce over it, or serve the sauce on the side. Makes about 1/4 cup of sauce, so double it if you want more.
---
From Mum. Best served with fava beans and a nice Chianti.

=== Potato Dumplings with Pork Roast and Sauerkraut | German | 6 | 180 | celebration | 02, 03
6 medium potatoes
1 egg
1/2 tsp salt
1 cup flour
6 pieces white bread, toasted and cut in 1/2 inch cubes
4 lb pork shoulder roast
salt and pepper
1 jar refrigerated sauerkraut
4 packets McCormick pork gravy mix
---
Pork roast: heat the oven to 325°F. Salt and pepper all sides of the roast. Sear all sides in a Dutch oven on the stove.
Roast uncovered until the inside reaches 155 to 160°F, about 20 to 30 minutes per pound. It should have a hint of pink in the center.
Take the roast out, tent loosely with foil for 10 to 15 minutes, and slice against the grain.
Dumplings: boil the potatoes with the peel on until soft. Peel and let them cool.
Whip the potatoes with a mixer, add the egg and salt, then add the flour slowly while beating. The amount varies; the mix shouldn't be too dry or sticky.
Form balls about 2 1/2 inches across, push a piece of toast into the middle of each, and reform.
Bring a very large pot of water to a full boil and drop the dumplings in one at a time without crowding. Use more pots rather than crowd them. Cook 8 minutes.
Keep the dumplings in the water until serving, but not too long or they fall apart.
Sauerkraut: empty the jar into a pan and heat over medium until it begins to boil.
Make the gravy (1 packet makes 1 cup) and serve it over the dumplings.
---
Passed from mother to daughter for decades in the Gallagher and Mahaffey families. Gravy is a must for the dumplings. Enjoy, Poppy.

=== Dank Curry | Thai | 6 | 45 |  | 05
1 tbsp olive oil
1 medium yellow onion, chopped small
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
2 tbsp curry powder
2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed small
2 cups cauliflower florets
2 cups broccoli florets
2 medium carrots
4 red bell peppers, sliced
3/4 cup lentils
2 cans coconut milk
2 cups vegetable broth
2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
1 tbsp cornstarch
2 limes, sliced
3 cups fresh baby spinach
1/2 bunch cilantro, chopped (optional)
1 cup jasmine rice
---
Grab two pots, a big one (A) for the curry and a smaller one (B) for the rice.
Heat the olive oil in pot A on medium.
Put 2 cups of water in pot B, cover, and bring to a boil on high.
Add the garlic and ginger to pot A and stir until fragrant.
When pot B boils, turn it to the lowest heat, add 1 cup of rice, cover, and don't touch it until it's fluffy.
Add the cauliflower, broccoli and onion to pot A and cook about 7 minutes, until slightly cooked.
Add the coconut milk, vegetable broth, curry paste and curry powder.
Stir, add the sweet potatoes, carrots and lentils, and let it go for 15 minutes or until the lentils are done.
Add the bell peppers, the cornstarch if it's too thin, and the spinach. Simmer 5 more minutes.
Scoop rice into bowls, pour the curry over and garnish with cilantro and lime.
---
The card says 1/2 to 1 cup lentils and "a full bulb" of garlic if you're feeling it.

=== Tex-Mex Enchiladas con Chili con Carne | Mexican | 6 | 150 | celebration | 08, 09
2 lb chuck steak
3 cloves garlic, crushed
1 tsp salt
1 tsp cayenne
5 tbsp chili powder
5 tbsp masa harina
4 cups hot water
1 1/2 tbsp vinegar
2 cubes beef bouillon
4 tbsp oil
2 packages corn tortillas
2 tbsp flour
2 tbsp chili powder
1/2 tsp salt
1 cup water
2 onions, chopped
1 lb cheese (American and cheddar)
---
Chili, made first: cut the meat into 1/2 inch cubes and sauté until lightly browned, adding a little oil if needed.
Add the garlic, salt, cayenne and 5 tbsp chili powder. Mix well and let the seasoning soak into the meat for a few minutes.
Sprinkle the masa harina over the meat and mix thoroughly. Add the hot water, vinegar and bouillon.
Lower the heat, cover and simmer until the meat is tender and some of it dissolves into the chili. Add a little water if it gets dry and adjust the seasoning.
Enchiladas: heat a little oil in a skillet and soften each tortilla, one at a time.
Make a chili sauce by stirring the flour, 2 tbsp chili powder and 1/2 tsp salt into 2 tbsp oil. Add the water and blend until smooth.
On each tortilla put some cheese and onion, drizzle with the sauce, roll, and place in a greased baking dish.
Pour the rest of the sauce over, sprinkle with the remaining cheese and onion.
Bake at 350°F until hot and the cheese is melted, about 20 to 30 minutes. Serve with the chili con carne ladled on top.
---
From the kitchen of the Blount family, originally from the "Texas the Beautiful Cookbook". Buy a hunk of American cheese from the deli and grate it; this makes very creamy enchiladas.

=== Dutch Oven Chuck Stew | American | 4 | 120 |  | 10
4 lb chuck roast
1/2 cup flour, as needed
3 tbsp Crisco, as needed
2 cloves garlic, diced
1/2 cup celery, cut in 1/2 inch lengths
1 can diced tomatoes (14.5 oz), half the liquid drained
1 bottle beer (stout, not light beer)
3 potatoes, peeled and quartered
1 medium onion, diced
3 carrots, peeled and cut in 1/2 inch lengths
1 large turnip, peeled and quartered
1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
1/4 tsp dried thyme (optional)
1 tsp salt
1 tsp ground pepper
---
Cut the chuck roast into 1 1/2 inch cubes, coat with flour and set aside.
Melt Crisco in the Dutch oven to about 1/16 inch deep. When hot, cook the onions until soft, then the garlic for about 1 minute without burning it.
Take the onions and garlic out and set them on a paper towel. Add more oil if needed, to 1/16 to 1/8 inch.
When the oil is very hot, brown the meat in batches without crowding. Pour off extra oil afterward.
Return the onions and garlic and add the celery, carrots, potatoes, turnip, tomatoes, beer, Worcestershire, thyme, and salt to taste.
Cook with the lid on about 1 to 1 1/2 hours, until the meat is tender and the vegetables are soft. Stir now and then, adding more beer if it isn't covering the stew.
At the end, thicken with flour mixed with water if needed.
---
From the outdoor ventures of Poppy. Makes enough for 4. Good with Pillsbury Grands biscuits baked in a second Dutch oven stacked on top. Campfire note: one Kingsford briquette is about 10 degrees; put about 1/4 of the coals on the bottom and 3/4 on top, and turn the oven a quarter turn every 10 minutes.

=== Chicken with Mustard Mascarpone Marsala Sauce | Italian | 4 | 20 | fast | 11
1 lb cooked chicken or turkey breast, chopped
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp butter
1/2 cup chopped onion
1 cup sliced cremini mushrooms
3 cloves garlic, minced
2/3 cup Marsala wine
1/3 cup mascarpone cheese
2 tbsp Dijon mustard
2 tbsp minced fresh parsley
---
Heat the oil and butter in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and sauté until tender, about 2 minutes.
Add the mushrooms and sauté 5 minutes, stirring often. Add the garlic and stir 30 seconds until fragrant.

Stir in the parsley and serve.
---
From the Bouchers: super easy and oh so delicious. Luca loves this one over jasmine rice. A good way to use leftover chicken.

=== Drunken Chicken | Other | 4 | 70 |  | 12

1 can tomatoes (16 oz), with liquid

1/4 tsp ground cloves
1/4 cup brown sugar

---

=== Stuffed Cabbage Rolls | German | 6 | 100 |  | 14
1 head cabbage
1 onion, chopped
3 tbsp butter
1 can stewed tomatoes
1 can tomato sauce
1 clove garlic, minced
1/4 cup instant rice
1 lb ground beef
1/2 tsp onion powder
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/2 tsp allspice
1 jar sauerkraut
1/4 cup brown sugar
1 pint sour cream
---
Steam the cabbage leaves until soft, then cool.

=== Roasted Salmon with Kale and Cabbage | American | 4 | 30 | fast | 17

2 tbsp lemon juice
1/4 cup dill, chopped
1 tsp Dijon mustard

---

=== Zuppa Toscana | Italian | 8 | 60 | soup | 37
1 lb bulk Italian sausage
1 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
4 slices bacon, cut into 1/2 inch pieces
1 large onion, diced
1 tbsp minced garlic
5 cans chicken broth
6 potatoes, thinly sliced
1 cup heavy cream
2 cups fresh spinach
---
Cook the sausage and red pepper flakes in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat until crumbly, browned and no longer pink, 10 to 15 minutes. Drain and set aside.
Cook the bacon in the same Dutch oven over medium-high heat until crisp. Drain, leaving 2 tbsp drippings with the bacon in the pan.
Stir in the onions and garlic and cook until the onions are soft, 5 minutes.
Pour the broth into the pot with the bacon and onion mixture and bring to a boil over high heat.
Add the potatoes and boil until fork tender, 20 minutes.
Reduce heat to medium, stir in the cream and cooked sausage, and heat through.
Mix in the spinach just before serving.
---
From the kitchen of Auntie Pattie. Sausage can be mild or spicy. Broth cans are 13.75 oz.

=== Chicken Piccata | Italian | 4 | 30 |  | 39
1/4 cup flour
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp pepper
4 boneless chicken breast halves (4 oz each)
1/4 cup butter, cubed
1/4 cup white wine or chicken broth
1 tbsp lemon juice
---
In a large resealable plastic bag combine the flour, salt and pepper.
Flatten the chicken to 1/2 inch thickness. Place in the bag one piece at a time and shake to coat.
In a large skillet brown the chicken over medium heat in the butter.
Stir in the wine or broth and bring to a boil.

=== Homemade Ramen | Japanese | 4 | 50 | soup | web
2 tbsp olive oil, divided
3 tbsp butter, divided
8 oz mushrooms, sliced (baby bella)
3/4 lb chicken breast (1 large, boneless and skinless)
salt and pepper
1/2 cup dry white wine
3 cloves garlic, minced
6 cups low sodium chicken broth
2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
2 tsp hot sauce (Frank's)
2 tsp honey
3/4 tsp toasted sesame oil
2 packets instant ramen noodles (3 oz each), no flavor packet
6 leaves bok choy, roughly chopped
3/4 tsp onion powder
3/4 tsp mustard powder
1/4 tsp ground ginger
1/8 tsp white pepper
1 pinch red pepper flakes
green onions, for serving
honey roasted peanuts, roughly chopped, for serving
4 eggs, soft boiled, for serving
---
Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large soup pot over medium-high heat. Sauté the mushrooms until golden, about 4 minutes. Remove and set aside.
Slice the chicken in half lengthwise to make 2 thinner pieces. Cover with plastic wrap and pound to 1/2 inch thick with the textured side of a meat mallet.
Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
Heat the remaining olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in the pot over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken in batches 4 to 5 minutes per side until golden. Set aside, turn off the heat, rest 10 minutes, then cut into strips.
Add the wine and set the heat to medium. Scrape the bottom and sides of the pot, and let the wine bubble gently and reduce by half, 4 to 5 minutes.
Add the last 1 tbsp butter and the garlic and cook 2 minutes.
Add the broth, soy sauce, hot sauce, honey, sesame oil and seasonings. Bring to a gentle boil and let it reduce for 10 minutes while you soft boil the eggs.
Soft boiled eggs: cover the eggs with 2 inches of cool water, bring gradually to a gentle boil, then cook 6 minutes. Rinse under cool water, peel and halve.
Bring the soup to a rapid boil, add the ramen and cook 1 minute. Reduce to a gentle simmer, add the bok choy, mushrooms and chicken, and simmer until the noodles are done, about 3 minutes.
Serve topped with green onions, chopped peanuts and a soft boiled egg.
---
From The Cozy Cook (Stephanie): https://thecozycook.com/wprm_print/homemade-ramen
Any instant ramen works (Maruchan or Top Ramen). Keep the broth and soy sauce low sodium because the noodles add salt; if you only have regular broth, swap 1 to 2 cups of it for water.
Cremini mushrooms are best, but white button work, or leave them out. Sauvignon Blanc is the best wine, then Chardonnay or Pinot Grigio; chicken broth works instead.
Kale or spinach can stand in for bok choy. Keeps 3 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.
